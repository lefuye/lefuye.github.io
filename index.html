who is lefye

lefye; streaming as lefye_ (lefye_ on Twitch) is a Canadian content creator covering Genshin Impact (sometimes games like Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, or Wuthering Waves, more to come) across Twitch, YouTube, and TikTok. Content centers on gameplay, exploration, talking to chat, alongside community-run Discord tools. Off stream, lefye mostly doomscrolls, but sometimes likes to build self-hosted Discord bots and is self-taught in Blender, still learning the ropes on Genshin-based 3D projects, and runs a Linux home server (Debian btw). Music interests lean toward breakcore, IDM, and hyperpop; particularly the Sewerslvt and Cynthoni lineage of internet-native electronic music.
